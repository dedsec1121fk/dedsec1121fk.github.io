# Inspect suspicious files without execution

1. Never run an unknown executable on your everyday phone just to observe its behavior.

2. Record filename, extension, size, source and hash in an isolated research note.

3. Inspect metadata and readable headers with reputable tools; do not trust embedded URLs.

4. Compare alleged publisher claims with official signed release information.

5. Exercise: examine a harmless file's metadata and explain what metadata cannot prove.

**Scope:** Education and testing on owned or explicitly authorized systems only.
