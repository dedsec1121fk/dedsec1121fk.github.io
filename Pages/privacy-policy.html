Learn about our privacy practices and how we protect your data.

6. Cookies And Similar Technologies

The website uses one necessary first-party preference cookie named dedsec_cookie_consent_v1 to remember whether analytics were accepted or declined for up to 12 months. On ded-sec.space, this preference is shared with its subdomains so the consent banner does not reappear separately on each subdomain.

Google Tag Manager and any Google Analytics measurement are not loaded unless the visitor actively accepts analytics. If accepted, the active Google configuration may use cookies such as _ga, _ga_*, _gid, or _gat* and may measure page URL and title, referrer, visit time, configured clicks or interactions, browser/device information, and approximate region-level data. Advertising storage and ad-personalization consent remain denied by the website’s consent configuration.

If analytics are declined, the Google Tag Manager script is not requested by the website and analytics cookies are not created by this website. Visitors can reopen “Cookie settings” on any page to review or change their choice. Google Search Console does not require a visitor-tracking script.

7. Data Retention

Technical records and communications are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, operational, legal, moderation, or record-keeping purposes, after which they may be deleted, anonymized, or minimized where appropriate.

8. Security

Reasonable steps may be taken to protect technical and communication data, but no website, transmission method, or storage environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure or uninterrupted.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent where consent applies. To make a privacy-related request, use the contact methods listed on the Contact & Credits page.

10. International Transfers

11. Children's Privacy

The website and tools are not directed at children under 13. If we become aware that personal data of a child under 13 has been collected in a way covered by COPPA or other applicable law, reasonable steps will be taken to delete it.
